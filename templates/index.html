What is Talao Wallet?

Talao Wallet is a production-ready, open-source Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) wallet available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Since its launch in 2022, the wallet code has been downloaded more than 50,000 times, making it one of the most widely deployed SSI wallets in the ecosystem.

Talao Wallet enables users to securely store, manage and present Verifiable Credentials (VCs), giving them full control over their digital identity.

The wallet supports major digital identity ecosystems including:

  • EUDI Wallet ecosystem (European Digital Identity)
  • EBSI (European Blockchain Services Infrastructure)
  • MOSIP (Modular Open Source Identity Platform)
  • DIIP (Decentralized Identity Interoperability Profile)
  • Other Self-Sovereign Identity ecosystems

Standards & Credential Formats

Talao Wallet supports multiple Verifiable Credential formats, ensuring interoperability across ecosystems:

  • IETF SD-JWT VC
  • W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model
  • OpenBadge VC format

It also supports the full evolution of the OpenID for Verifiable Credentials (OIDC4VC) specifications — from early drafts to the latest finalized 1.0 specifications.

Customizable for Each Ecosystem

Talao Wallet can be configured to adhere to a specific ecosystem profile. This makes it possible to simulate, test or deploy wallet configurations tailored to EUDI, EBSI or other SSI frameworks.

The wallet includes:

  • Advanced configuration capabilities
  • Developer Mode for debugging and inspection
  • Protocol-level diagnostics though AI Agents
  • Integrated issuer links for rapid credential issuance

A specific release of the wallet, named Altme, has also been developed to support both digital identity and crypto features. This version is particularly well suited for Web3 use cases, combining verifiable credential capabilities with blockchain-based wallet functionalities. More information is available at altme.io.

These features make Talao Wallet particularly suitable for testing within environments such as the EUDI Wallet Playground.
